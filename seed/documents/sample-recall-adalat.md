# SAMPLE Recall Notice No. S-002/2026: ADALAT LA 30MG TABLETS, batch SEED-A4-2374-B

SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING AND DEMONSTRATION. This is not an official NAFDAC publication; the product details below are illustrative.

## Reason for the recall

The registration holder is recalling one batch of ADALAT LA 30MG TABLETS (NAFDAC Reg. No: A4-2374), Batch No: SEED-A4-2374-B, because dissolution testing found results outside the approved specification.

## Action required

Distributors, pharmacies and hospitals should stop supplying the batch, quarantine remaining stock and return it to the registration holder. Other batches of the product are not affected by this recall.
