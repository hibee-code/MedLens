# SAMPLE Standard Operating Procedure: reporting a suspected counterfeit medicine

SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING AND DEMONSTRATION. This is not an official NAFDAC procedure.

## When to report

Report a pack when its NAFDAC number is not in the register, its batch number is not one the manufacturer declared, the printing or packaging differs from genuine packs, or a published alert or recall names its batch.

## How to report

Keep the pack and its receipt. Record the product name, NAFDAC number, batch number, expiry date, where and when it was bought, and the supplier. Send the report to the nearest NAFDAC office or the state pharmaceutical inspectorate, and do not return the pack to the supplier.

## After reporting

A regulator may collect the pack for laboratory testing. Laboratory results decide whether the product is counterfeit; until then, treat it as suspicious and do not dispense it.
