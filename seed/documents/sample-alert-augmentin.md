# SAMPLE Public Alert No. S-001/2026: counterfeit Augmentin 1 G in circulation

SAMPLE DOCUMENT FOR TESTING AND DEMONSTRATION. This is not an official NAFDAC publication; the product details below are illustrative.

## Summary

The agency alerts the public to counterfeit Augmentin 1 G tablets found in Lagos and Ogun States. The counterfeit packs carry NAFDAC Reg. No: 04-8655 and Batch No: AUG-FAKE-2611, a batch the registration holder did not manufacture.

## Details of the counterfeit product

Product name: Augmentin 1 G. NAFDAC Reg. No: 04-8655. Batch No: AUG-FAKE-2611. Expiry date: 11/2027. The outer carton has blurred printing and the blister foil carries no batch number.

## Advice to healthcare providers

Pharmacists and patent medicine vendors should check the batch number before dispensing, quarantine any pack with the batch above, and report it to the nearest NAFDAC office or through the agency's reporting channels.
